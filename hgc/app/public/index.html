      A SMALL GUIDE
      
Two functions. One shader.

      

        Pick a shape in SDF function and a look in
        Visualization. Both templates are combined into the
        live preview.
      

      

        
float sdf(vec2 p)

        

          Return the distance from p to your shape. Negative is
          inside; zero is the boundary. Coordinates span −1 to 1 along the
          shorter canvas side.
        

        
vec3 shade(vec2 p, float d)

        

          Turn the signed distance d into an RGB color. You can
          call sdf() here, too.
        

      

      

        u_timeTime in secondsu_resolutionCanvas size, vec2u_durationLoop length in secondsu_frameFrame number, int
      

      

        For a seamless loop, animate with
        sin(6.283185 * u_time / u_duration). Exports sample the
        interval from zero up to the loop end, excluding the duplicate final
        frame.

        Save a copy adds a new template to the server library.
        Compiler errors keep the last valid preview visible.
